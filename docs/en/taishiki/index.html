The Taishiki-chō scale

Taishiki-chō uses the ryo scale. Its five core degrees are kyū E, shō F#, kaku G#, chi B, u C#, with ei-kaku A and ei-u D as the two additional degrees. The characters on the tonic are played steadily at standard pitch, without meri.
